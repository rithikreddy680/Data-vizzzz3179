Queensland Crash Locations, Seasonality, and LGA Hotspots

Notes and assumptions

  • Casualties are integers; the filter buckets are derived using floor values (0–1, 2–3, 4–5, 6+).
  • Year range controls filter all visuals; severity legend selection is shared.
  • Monthly totals are annotated with mean and the peak month to highlight seasonality.
  • LGA labels on the map indicate the 10 highest-crash LGAs by current filters; bar chart shows top 15.
  • Data source: crash_data_queensland_1_crash_locations.csv.
